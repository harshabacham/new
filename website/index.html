Public Awareness

Citizen ATM Safety & Fraud Prevention Guide

Important steps to protect your hard-earned money and help prevent cybercrime cash withdrawals.

The Golden Hour Rule

If you fall victim to financial fraud, call 1930 immediately within the first 1-2 hours. This allows cyber teams to place a freeze on beneficiary bank accounts before cash is withdrawn at an ATM.

Watch for Multi-Card Sweeps

Money mules often carry dozens of cloned or rented debit cards and make back-to-back withdrawals. If you spot suspicious repeated ATM withdrawals, alert the security guard or police beat.

Inspect ATM Slots

Before inserting your card, check if the card reader slot feels loose, glued, or protruding. Look for hidden pinhole cameras above the keypad designed to record your PIN.

Never Share Mule Accounts

Never rent or allow strangers to use your bank account or UPI QR code for a "commission". Facilitating mule transactions is a punishable offense under the IT Act and Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.
