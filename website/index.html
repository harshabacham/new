Architecture & Methodology

How CyberWatch Forecasts Cash Withdrawals

A 3-stage spatiotemporal machine learning system trained on historical cybercrime complaints, spatial ATM densities, and money mule transit patterns.

1

1930 Cybercrime Telemetry

Ingests real-time complaint data from the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (NCRP / 1930) and extracts scam category, amount, and initial transaction timestamps.

2

Spatiotemporal AI Models

A Champion Stacked Ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees and calibrated probability estimators forecasts withdrawal likelihood across 400 discrete 1km x 1km quadrants.

3

Tactical Patrol Dispatch

Law enforcement receives ranked priority sectors, optimal patrol shift windows (e.g. 14:00 - 22:00), and automated legal documentation for bank CCTV preservation.
